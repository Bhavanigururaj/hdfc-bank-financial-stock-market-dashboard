HDFC Bank – Power BI Financial Analysis Dashboard

Dashboard Pages

1. Executive Dashboard: Provides a high-level overview of HDFC Bank’s key financial and market indicators, including NII, Other Income, profitability, deposits, advances, DPS, EPS, asset quality, ROA, ROE, and stock prices.

2. Financial Insights Dashboard: Highlights key performance indicators such as Total Income, PAT, Total Deposits, Total Advances, ROE, Latest Stock Price, Income Trend, and PAT Trend.

3. Performance Trends: Analyses Total Income, Total Asset Growth, GNPA vs NNPA, Stock Price Trend, NIM, Cost-to-Income Ratio, Market Capitalisation, NII, CASA Ratio, and Monthly Returns.

4. Relationship Analysis: Examines the relationship between EPS and Stock Price, ROE and Stock Price, and NII and Stock Price to understand financial performance and market valuation.

5. Comparative Performance Analysis: Compares Operating Expenses with Total Income, PAT with Stock Price, and Profitability with Income to evaluate financial and market performance.

6. Analysis & Conclusion: Presents key findings, suggestions, and an overall conclusion based on the analysis of HDFC Bank’s financial and market performance.

This dashboard provides a data-driven view of HDFC Bank’s financial health, profitability, growth, asset quality, operational efficiency, shareholder returns, and stock market performance, helping users understand key trends and relationships through interactive Power BI visualizations.

Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Microsoft Excel
- Financial and market data
